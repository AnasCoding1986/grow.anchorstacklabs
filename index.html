How a reel is built

The anatomy of a 30-second reel

Every reel we make follows the same four parts. The timing changes with the length, but the order doesn't.

0–2 sHookThe question your customer is already asking.
2–12 sOfferWhat you sell, what it costs, and what's included.
12–22 sProofReal details: your photos and clips, location, hours, what makes you reliable.
22–30 sActionHow to reach you, large on screen, with time to read it.

It works on mute. Most people watch reels with the sound off, so every key word also appears on screen. The voice-over adds to the reel, but it isn't needed to understand it.

Questions

Things you might be wondering

What exactly do I receive?

A vertical full-HD video file, ready to post on Facebook and Instagram Reels, plus the caption text to go with it. Premium also includes a cover image.

Can I see samples first?

Yes. Email or WhatsApp us and we'll send you reels we've made.

I don't have good photos. Is that a problem?

No. Phone photos and short phone clips are enough. We'll tell you exactly what to shoot and how.

We're in different time zones. How does that work?

Everything is written: the script, the changes, the delivery. You reply when it suits you, and we reply within 24 hours on business days.

What if I don't like the reel?

You approve the script before we start, so the reel says what you expect. After that, you get 1 to 3 rounds of changes depending on the package.

Will the reel have music?

Usually not a song. We build reels around the voice and ambient sound, so attention stays on your message and there are no copyright problems.

Can I start with one reel and choose a monthly plan later?

Yes, and we recommend it. Start with one reel, see how it does, then decide on a monthly plan.
